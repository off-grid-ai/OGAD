# What the chat upgrade owes a person (w41 contract)

Seven steps, in the order a person lives them. One acceptance line each.

1. A person updates Desktop and opens it, and has nothing old saved.
   ACCEPTANCE: no upgrade box appears at all, and the app comes up fully usable.

2. A person opens Desktop with old chats and projects saved, and the upgrade succeeds.
   ACCEPTANCE: the box shows progress, then closes by itself, and afterwards every
   project and every chat they had is still there and still openable.

3. The upgrade is running.
   ACCEPTANCE: the person is told it is running and how much is being copied, and the
   app never looks broken or half-started while it works.

4. The upgrade cannot finish this time.
   ACCEPTANCE: the person is told in their own words what happened, not in the
   database's words, and told plainly that nothing of theirs was removed.

5. The person tries again.
   ACCEPTANCE: trying again is only offered when it could actually succeed. Each
   attempt starts from the same untouched saved chats, so trying again never makes
   things worse.

6. The upgrade can never finish, however many times they try.
   ACCEPTANCE: the person is not offered a button that cannot work. They are given
   one thing they can really do - keep using the app on their old chats, or send
   the details for repair - and they are never trapped behind the box.

7. In every case above, the person still has an app.
   ACCEPTANCE: the rest of Desktop starts and works. Chat, models, dictation, tasks
   and memory never come up silently dead. If a part truly cannot work, the person
   is told on screen, not only in a log.

In every single case: the person's existing chats and projects are untouched. The
upgrade only ever adds a new copy. Nothing of theirs is deleted, renamed, emptied or
partly written, whether the upgrade succeeds, fails once, or fails forever.

## The line tonight's app breaks

Step 2. A person with old chats and projects opens Desktop, and the upgrade fails
instead of succeeding, so they never reach their chats. Step 6 and step 7 break as a
consequence: they are handed a Retry button that can never succeed, and the whole app
is dead behind the box while saying nothing about it.
