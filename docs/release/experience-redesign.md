# What's new: Off Grid AI Desktop stops working so hard to look alive

Every screen looks the way it did, but your Mac does far less work to draw it.

## Settings

**Animations off covers the new light too.**
- Turn off Hover and click animations in Settings, UI, and cards stop lighting up under your pointer.

## The look, everywhere

**Moving your pointer no longer keeps your Mac busy.**
- On Models, a moving pointer used to cost about 950 ms of work every second. Now it's about 200.
- At rest, the glowing grid behind every page uses 3 to 5 ms of work a second. It used 7 to 12.

**The light still follows you.**
- The card under your pointer still lights its edge, and the dots around it still glow.
- The dots don't lean away from the pointer any more. The card's text stays exactly as it was.

**One click, one ripple.**
- Press Refresh in Setup & health and the page ripples once. Before, every status line that changed sent its own wave, so a single click kept rippling.

**Colours and type match your phone.**
- Desktop now takes its colours and fonts from the same source as Off Grid AI on mobile and on the web, so they stay in step.
